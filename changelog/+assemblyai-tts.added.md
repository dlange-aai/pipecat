- Added `AssemblyAITTSService`, for AssemblyAI's Streaming TTS WebSocket API. Each sentence is sent as its own request on a single session, interruptions cancel in-flight synthesis without reconnecting, and per-word timings drive word-level `TTSTextFrame` output. The session's `language` is derived from the preset voice unless set, and the US and EU data-residency hosts are available through `url`. See `examples/voice/voice-assemblyai-tts.py`.
